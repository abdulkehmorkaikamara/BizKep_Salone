07
Trust by design

Security and accountability are part of every workflow

AUTH

Individual authentication

Strong password hashing, secure sessions, account lockouts, Turnstile bot protection, and authenticator OTP owner recovery.

ROLE

Server-enforced permissions

Owner, Manager, and Attendant permissions are checked by the Worker—not trusted to the browser.

LOG

Append-only evidence

Inventory and audit records cannot be silently edited or deleted after they are written.

TEN

Business isolation

Every stock item, sale, staff member, expense, and report is scoped to its own business workspace.
